The audit's spatial-alignment question cannot be closed with imagery alone. Two 2021 UrbIS flight products were co-registered on 15 fixed windows (`docs/imagery-registration-check.md`): one offset per window is stable to ~0.09 m across the finest bands, but the offset itself is 0.55-5.43 m (median 1.92 m) and varies along a street, so the difference is local (mosaic/resection or relief), not a single georeferencing constant.

Remaining gap: no accuracy assessment for either product. Options:
1. Ask CIRB/Paradigm (or the publisher of the VUB 2021 vegetation product) for documented positional-accuracy metadata (RMSE, GCP residuals, tiling/resection reports).
2. If none is available, run a control-point assessment on clearly identifiable ground-level features, reporting residuals plus reference uncertainty, and validate on points not used for tuning.

Do not present the product-to-product offset as an error bound: both products may share a systematic error. Lane-level overhead-canopy claims along the reference lines remain uncalibrated until this is resolved (see `data/research/cycling-canopy-checkpoint.md`).
