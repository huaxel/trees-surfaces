## Independent extraction (the doc's prescribed check) agrees exactly

`netCDF4`/`h5py` with dimension-name indexing and automatic scaling disabled reproduced the tool's reference-day and JJA values for all 39 cells to **5e-5 °C** (the tool's rounding limit), and independently found the same minimum difference (+0.23 °C, cell 136:168, 2016-05-30). Two traps were hit and are now recorded in `docs/wbgt-mean-max-method.md`: library dimension order differs (`HDF5.jl` vs `h5py`/`netCDF4`), and `netCDF4` applies `scale_factor` automatically (double-scaling gave a 100× error).
